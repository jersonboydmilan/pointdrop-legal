What PointDrop is

PointDrop transfers a single, verified destination from one phone to another using a short-lived code. It is not a maps app, a booking service, a ride-hailing service, or a messaging app, and does not arrange, broker, or guarantee any transportation.

Accuracy is the sender's responsibility

The app shows you the destination you are about to send — its name, address, and exact coordinates — before you send it. You are responsible for confirming it is correct. [Operator name] is not responsible for consequences of sending an incorrect destination.

No guarantee of delivery

A code may expire, be scanned by the wrong person, or never be scanned at all. Where a receiver page is deployed, the app shows you when a code has been opened, but this is a best-effort signal, not a guarantee. Where none is deployed, the code is a plain Google Maps link: there is no expiry, no "opened" signal, and nothing to cancel, and the send screen says so.

Acceptable use

Do not use PointDrop to send destinations for unlawful purposes, to harass or endanger another person, or to attempt to abuse, overload, or reverse engineer the service. We may delete a stored destination, or restrict access to the service, in response to abuse — but a Google Maps link already handed to someone is outside anyone's reach, ours included.

No account, no warranty of continuity

There is no account to lose access to. We do not promise the service will be available at any given time, and reserve the right to change or discontinue it.

Third-party services

Navigation happens entirely inside third-party apps (Google Maps, Apple Maps, Waze, or whichever the recipient chooses) once they leave the receiver page — or, where no receiver page is deployed, the moment they scan the code, which opens Google Maps directly. Their own terms govern that navigation. Map tiles and, where configured, place search are provided by third parties described in the Privacy Policy.
