Privacy Policy for PointDrop

Effective Date: September 12, 2026 Last Updated: September 12, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Jerson Boyd Milan ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile application PointDrop (the "App"), available on Google Play.

The App is designed to be simple and user-friendly. Its core functions are:

  • Finding a location
  • Generating a QR code
  • Sending that QR code to a driver

Important: The App does not require user accounts or logins. We do not create user profiles, and we do not build a history of where you have been.

2. Information We Process

Because the App has no login or account system, we collect and retain very little information.

Location data. The App uses your device's location services (with your permission) solely to help you find and select a location so a QR code can be generated.

  • Location is processed on your device and, when needed, temporarily sent through our backend to our location provider (Geoapify) to return place information. Your search text travels the same way. Neither is stored or logged by us.
  • We do not store your location history or continuous tracking data.

The destination you choose to send. When you create a code, the destination's name, address and coordinates are stored on our server so the code can be resolved, together with a one-way hash of the code itself — never the code your driver scans. A scheduled purge deletes the destination and the code shortly after it expires, at most about 61 minutes after creation.

Microphone. The App offers voice search: when you tap the microphone button, your device's speech recognition service listens and converts your speech to text, which is then used as your search query. The microphone is used only while you are actively using that feature, and only after you have granted permission. We do not record, store or transmit audio ourselves, and the App never listens in the background. You can use every part of the App by typing instead, and denying the microphone permission does not affect anything else.

A random installation identifier. Every place search costs money, so searches are limited per installation. The first time you search, the App generates a random string and keeps it in its own storage until you uninstall the App. It is not derived from your device, your accounts, or your advertising ID, it is sent only with search requests, and our server keeps only a one-way hash of it next to a request count.

Other technical data. When the App communicates with our backend (hosted on Supabase) or location services, standard technical information such as IP address, device type and operating system version may be processed transiently by those providers to deliver the service. Our server uses the IP address only to limit how many requests one address may make, keeping a shortened hash of it next to a count that the same purge deletes. We do not use this information to identify you.

We do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact details
  • Account credentials (there are none)
  • Photos, contacts, or files from your device
  • Advertising identifiers for tracking
  • Analytics or usage profiles that identify you

3. How We Use Information

We use the limited information described above only to:

  • Display relevant location results
  • Generate the QR code you request
  • Transmit the QR code to the driver you designate
  • Maintain the basic functionality and security of the App

We do not use any information for advertising, profiling, marketing, or selling data.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the App:

  • Geoapify — location and places lookup. Involves your search text and, when you allow it, approximate coordinates.
  • Supabase — backend infrastructure. Involves transient technical data and the short-lived destination described above.
  • Google Maps — where the code you send is a Google Maps link, scanning it opens Google Maps on the driver's phone, and Google's own privacy policy governs what happens from that moment. We see nothing of it.
  • OpenStreetMap — map tiles shown inside the App.

These providers process data only as needed to provide their services and are bound by their own privacy policies. We do not sell or share personal information with them for their own marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

  • Location and QR-related data are processed only for the short time needed to complete your request. The stored destination and code are deleted automatically at most about 61 minutes after creation, by a purge that runs every 15 minutes.
  • We do not retain personal location history or user-identifiable records on our servers.
  • Your own list of searched, pinned and recently sent places is kept only on your device and is not sent to us. You can clear it in the App at any time.
  • Technical logs held by our service providers are retained only as long as necessary for security, debugging, and legal compliance, then deleted or anonymized.

Because we do not maintain user accounts, there is no personal profile to delete.

One thing outlives that window. Where the code you send is a Google Maps link, the destination's coordinates are inside the link itself. Deleting our copy does not unmake a link someone already holds, and such a link cannot be expired or revoked. Send a code only to the driver you mean to send it to.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings. The App will simply be unable to look up nearby places without it.
  • You can deny or revoke microphone permission at any time; voice search stops being offered and everything else works as before.
  • You can clear your local history of searched and pinned places from the App's home screen.
  • You can uninstall the App at any time; this removes all local data associated with it.
  • If you believe we hold any information about you and wish to request access, correction, or deletion, contact us using the details below. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through the App. All communication with our backend uses HTTPS, and codes are stored only as one-way hashes. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

9. International Users

The App is operated from the Philippines. If you access it from other regions, your information may be processed in countries that may have different data-protection laws than your own.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
